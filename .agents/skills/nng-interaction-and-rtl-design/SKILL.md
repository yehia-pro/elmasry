# NN/g Interaction & Arabic RTL Ergonomics

## Core Principles
1. **First-Class Arabic RTL & Typography**:
   - Proper typography with Cairo or Readex Pro fonts, natural line heights (`leading-relaxed`), and contextual Arabic numerals where appropriate.
   - Symmetric RTL padding, margins, flex directions, and chevron icon orientations (back/forward icons must mirror in RTL).
2. **Cognitive Load Reduction (Miller's Law & Hick's Law)**:
   - Break 30-field forms into sequential, clearly indicated progressive steps (Stepper).
   - Clear visual feedback for pending, approved, or rejected documents with actionable instructions on how to correct them.
3. **Mobile-First Touch Ergonomics**:
   - Touch targets must be at least 48x48px (Fitts's Law).
   - Primary action buttons (e.g., "تأكيد الحجز", "رفع المستند") pinned within the thumb zone on mobile screens.
4. **Physical-to-Digital Bridge (Medical Ticket / Card)**:
   - Clear, high-contrast, printable appointment card.
   - High-density QR code for instant scanner verification at the clinic door.
   - Bulleted checklist in bold Arabic: الأوراق والمستندات المطلوبة يوم الكشف، العنوان، الموعد بالساعة والدقيقة.
