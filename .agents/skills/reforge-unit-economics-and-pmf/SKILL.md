# Reforge Unit Economics & Product-Market Fit (PMF)

## Core Principles
1. **True Retention Curve Flattening**: A feature is not a product until the cohort retention curve flattens asymptotically.
2. **LTV / CAC Guardrails**:
   - In emerging/price-sensitive markets (Egypt/MENA), CAC must be minimized via organic loops (WhatsApp sharing, official institutional mandates, QR verification).
   - Target LTV / CAC > 3.0x.
   - Payback period < 6 months.
3. **Frictionless Onboarding**:
   - Low bandwidth tolerance: Every kilobyte counts. Optimize for 3G/4G budget Android devices.
   - Zero redundant steps: Do not ask for information that can be inferred (e.g., date of birth, age, and governorate can be extracted from the Egyptian 14-digit National ID).
4. **Moat & Defensibility**:
   - Workflow lock-in: Institute administration, medical committee signatures, barcode verification.
   - Institutional Network Effects: As students register, medical records are aggregated, making the platform the single source of truth for the academic year.
